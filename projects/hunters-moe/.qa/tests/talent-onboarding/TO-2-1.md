# TO-2-1: Edit Profile has the anonymous / public radios with their explanations (existing talent, read-only)
- Фича/экран: /profile?edit=personal (dialog «עריכת פרופיל», tab «פרטים אישיים»)
- Тикеты: #1866b6, #a904f9
- Роль/аккаунт: talent (demo-talent-1, U-13, .qa/auth/talent.json)
- Устройство: desktop 1440×900
- Тип: позитив
- Предусловия: saved talent session. Read-only — do not change the radio, do not save. demo-talent-1 is public (kept its choice).
## Шаги
1. Open /profile?edit=personal.
2. Make sure tab «פרטים אישיים» is active (click it if not).
3. Find the profile type radio group `profile-visibility`.
4. Switch the site language (header language selector) to English, Русский, العربية in turn; look at the radio group each time; switch back to עברית.
## Ожидаемо
- Two radios in group `profile-visibility`: anonymous («פרופיל אנונימי») and public («פרופיל גלוי»); exactly one is checked; for demo-talent-1 — public.
- Each option has its own explanation of what it means for privacy. (#a904f9 п.3 «with the existing explanation of what each option means»; §36 AC05)
- In en/ru/ar the two radio labels and explanations are translated (no Hebrew letters left in them), `html[lang]` is en/ru/ar, `dir` is ltr/ltr/rtl. (#a904f9 п.4 «texts in he/en/ru/ar»)
- Language is returned to Hebrew at the end.
