## D-03 · Recalculate ledger baseline — GAP-BASELINE-CURRENT-MONTH, вариант A
- Статус: **принято** («decided gap»)
- Решение: «After a recalculation the first future month opens at Total Cash exactly and every later month chains from it, per decided gap GAP-BASELINE-CURRENT-MONTH option A.» Уточнение в задаче #49d1c5: «the seed month is the first future month opening at Total Cash exactly with the current month’s unpaid movements left in the current row (GAP-BASELINE-CURRENT-MONTH, option A)».
- Влияет: עמוד תזרים מזומנים → Monthly Cash Ledger, Liquidity Runway Forecast.
- Источник: «מפרט» → Feature 6aa28526a6de554b4f0f43fd «Recalculate ledger baseline from Total Cash»; задачи #49d1c5, #b52c64 (обе done).

## D-04 · Recalculate ledger baseline — GAP-BASELINE-MANUAL-OVERRIDES, вариант A
- Статус: **принято**
- Решение: «Months carrying a manual Opening Balance keep it, still win on their own month, and are named to the user as pinned in the confirmation and in the result, per GAP-BASELINE-MANUAL-OVERRIDES option A.»

## D-05 · Recalculate ledger baseline — GAP-BASELINE-PERSISTENCE, вариант A
- Статус: **принято**
- Решение: «The anchored amount, the actor and the timestamp are stored and shown on the table until the next recalculation, and the anchor is marked stale once live Total Cash differs from it, per GAP-BASELINE-PERSISTENCE option A.» В задаче #49d1c5: «a persisted, auditable as-of anchor shown until the next recalculation».
- Связанные правила той же фичи: «Months before the current calendar month are left unchanged by every recalculation»; «is unavailable with a stated reason whenever the session is not MFA-verified or no future month exists».
- Влияет: Monthly Cash Ledger (якорь, отметка stale), MFA-сессия.
- Источник: Feature 6aa28526a6de554b4f0f43fd; задача #49d1c5.
