## F-39 — Recalculate ledger baseline from Total Cash

- Спринт: — (в источнике пусто)
- Дизайн: design_status = none; design_links: 0; вложения: 0

**Описание:**

> The portfolio owner and the family’s finance viewers read the Monthly Cash Ledger to judge how much cash the portfolio will hold in each coming month. The table’s future openings are carried forward from the oldest stored ledger month, so they drift away from the money actually sitting in bank accounts and deposits, and the forecast quietly stops describing reality — while the Liquidity Runway block beside it already starts from current cash and therefore disagrees. This capability lets an authorised owner re-anchor the ledger’s future months to the portfolio’s current bank and deposit cash from the Cashflow page itself, and shows on the table which cash figure the forecast is standing on, who anchored it and when, so a reader can tell a current forecast from a stale one without leaving the page or asking anyone.

**User Story:** As the portfolio owner reading the Monthly Cash Ledger, I recalculate the baseline from Total Cash so that the coming months open at the money the portfolio actually holds and I can act on the forecast.

**AC:** AC нет (поле acceptance_criteria пустое).

**דרישות (demands), дословно:**

- After a recalculation the first future month opens at Total Cash exactly and every later month chains from it, per decided gap GAP-BASELINE-CURRENT-MONTH option A.
- An authorised user must be able to re-seed the ledger’s future months from current bank and deposit cash with one explicit, auditable action on the Cashflow table, rather than waiting for a silent background rule.
- An explicit recalculation action is offered on the Monthly Cash Ledger, and is unavailable with a stated reason whenever the session is not MFA-verified or no future month exists.
- Future months in the Monthly Cash Ledger must open from the portfolio’s actual bank and deposit cash (Total Cash) instead of chaining forward from the oldest ledger month’s stored opening, so the forecast a reader sees is anchored to real money.
- Months before the current calendar month are left unchanged by every recalculation, including their stored openings and manual overrides.
- Months carrying a manual Opening Balance keep it, still win on their own month, and are named to the user as pinned in the confirmation and in the result, per GAP-BASELINE-MANUAL-OVERRIDES option A.
- The anchored amount, the actor and the timestamp are stored and shown on the table until the next recalculation, and the anchor is marked stale once live Total Cash differs from it, per GAP-BASELINE-PERSISTENCE option A.
- The ledger’s first future opening and the Liquidity Runway Forecast’s starting cash agree after a recalculation in the All / Paid / Unpaid views, in every scenario and in the quarterly view.
